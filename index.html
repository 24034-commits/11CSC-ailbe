DOCFinder

This website is a place to view different DOC (Department of Conservation) sites in New Zealand and give ratings/recommendations for them. The purpose of this is to bring awareness to these sites and hopefully convince people to go outside and visit nature, and these sites, more.

What is DOC?

DOC, or the Department of Conservation, is New Zealand's primary government agency charged with conserving New Zealand's natural and historic heritage. They enforce laws, protect wildlife, manage public lands, and much more. They manage over 320 campsites and more than 950 backcountry huts across New Zealand, and you can book spots for most of them on the DOC booking page.
