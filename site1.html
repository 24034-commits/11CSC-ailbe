Tāwharanui

Tāwharanui is a campground and open sanctuary which is around an 80 to 90-minute drive from Auckland. It features a pearly-white sandy beaches, rolling pastures, and restored native coastal forests. It is protected by a 2.5km predator-proof fence, making it a safe haven for rare wildlife like brown Kiwi, Kākā, and Takahē. The beaches are perfect for swimming, surfing, and rock pooling, and there are plenty of trails to walk.

The main reason people come to Tāwharanui is to camp there. It is a perfect spot for camping, widely considered one of the best spots for camping near Auckland. Gates are open from 6:00 AM to 7:00 AM (and are extended to 9:00 PM during daylight saving hours). Camping is limited to tents. It has 4.9 stars on Google Maps with 2,555 reviews at the time of writing.
