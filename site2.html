Tāwharanui

Shakespear Regional Park is a regional park and scenic open sanctuary and working farm located at the tip of the Whangaparāoa Peninsula, about a 40-minute drive north of central Auckland. It features pest-free wildlife habitats, popular swimming beaches, and panoramic Hauraki Gulf views.

The main reason people come to Tāwharanui is to camp there. It is a perfect spot for camping, widely considered one of the best spots for camping near Auckland. Gates are open from 6:00 AM to 7:00 AM (and are extended to 9:00 PM during daylight saving hours). Camping is limited to tents. It has 4.9 stars on Google Maps with 2,555 reviews at the time of writing.
